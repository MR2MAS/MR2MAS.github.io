Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner's private Home Assistant server at home.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Google Calendar events, to show them on the home dashboard and send household reminders.
  • Where data goes: calendar data stays on the owner's own home server. It is not sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • Who uses it: only the owner's household. There are no other users.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke it at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party access.
